About Tiamin Group

Leading Manufacturing and Agricultural Company of the 21st Century, driven by dynamic zeal and an ambitious spirit.

Corporate Overview

From Road Construction to Global Trading Leadership

From our initial roots as a road construction company in 2011 to our latest strides on the national stage as a major contender in food processing and commodity farming; Tiamin has grown into one of the nation's leading manufacturing and agricultural companies of the 21st Century.

In the last 5 years, we have established a benchmark of churning out the finest home-grown rice and have incessantly strived to maintain the reputation of the company. The determined vision behind Tiamin group is to touch new heights and explore new horizons in agribusiness by delivering top quality products and services to our loyal consumers.

The company's multi-business portfolio encompasses a wide range of businesses from road construction, rice processing, farming, charcoal production, commodity exchange and even gold trading on the international platform (Dubai).

Leadership

Our Founder: Aminu Ahmed

In 2016, Aminu Ahmed embarked on a journey into the food processing business by setting up a 320 metric tonnes daily capacity rice mill plant in Kano. Barely 4 years later he expanded the processing capacity of his industry from 320 metric tonnes to 920 metric tons daily capacity, translating into 5.3 million bags of 50kg processed rice annually.

In order to feed the huge capacity of the rice mills, Aminu Ahmed is working with passion to cultivate a 10,000 hectares farmland in Bauchi state, north-eastern Nigeria. He is also working assiduously to establish the first functional Commodity Exchange in northern Nigeria to be known as Tiamin Commodity Exchange (TCX).

Aminu Ahmed has built a good reputation in the rice line of business and the agricultural commodity chain; such that his commitment, passion, and energy to work has always grown and motivated other people as well. This has made Tiamin a household name in Nigeria.
