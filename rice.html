Scale & Capacity

Our Strength & Infrastructure

State-of-the-art Mills

Tiamin Rice Ltd owns two massive rice mills located in Kano (Corporate HQ) and Bauchi. Kano has become a famous hub for producing the best quality raw, steam, and parboiled rice.

Our daily production capacity is 920 Metric tonnes (100% Sortex clean). The mills are equipped with the latest machinery and technology imported from China and India. Spanning 7 hectares, our facilities boast advanced processing, quality control, packaging, and warehousing units including stone separators, huskers, and multi-graders.

Farming & Procurement

About 40% of the raw material needed for our mills is derived from our 10,000-hectare irrigation farm at Udubo in Gamawa LGA of Bauchi State. We deploy ecosystem-based approaches designed to improve the sustainability of production systems and yield.

Our procurement involves deep understanding of the market, regular interaction with farmers, and engaging local farmers with inputs and extension services to ensure premium quality paddy right from the source.

Operations

Processing & Milling

Rice in its natural form is inedible. The main goal of our world-class milling is the precise removal of barns and hulls to make it beautifully polished and edible.

Drying Process

Fully mechanized and automatic gradual drying systems with online sensors control temperatures. This prevents breaking, bacterial infection, or fermentation, keeping the natural aroma intact.

Parboiling

Exposure to steam to remove the hull and barn layer. We use a committed Parboiling plant furnished with specially designed soaking bins and sensor-based water temperature controllers.

Milling

The final step exposes seed grains to varied sophisticated machines like Magnets, Paddy Separators, De-Huskers, and Precision Sizers to provide exceptionally refined and nutritious rice.

Sustainability

By-Products & Charcoal Briquettes

We are proud producers of 100% natural food-grade charcoal briquettes made from green material (rice husks). Processed from high-grade agricultural waste, they are used for starting and maintaining fires across various applications (BBQ, Incense, Hookah).

  • 100% Rice Husks Material
  • Odorless with Low Ash Content
  • Long Burning Time & Quick Ignition Time
  • Manufactured via steam/air activation technique at 800°C to 1000°C
