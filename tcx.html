Tiamin Commodity Exchange (TCX)

A regulated national and regional market linking buyers and sellers to discover prices and ensure market quality.

Transforming Markets

The Future of Agribusiness Trading

The Tiamin Commodity Exchange is a private company limited by shares incorporated to establish linkages between agricultural commodity producers and buyers, securing competitive prices, assuring market quantity and quality, and timely settlement of trades.

TCX key goal is to link Nigerian smallholder farmers to agricultural infrastructure, best quality farm inputs, and financial markets in Nigeria and across the region. We ensure farmers secure competitive prices for their commodities while supplying good quality commodities that meet the nutritional needs of the people.

We believe commodity exchanges are critical drivers of economic development and transformation. TCX offers tremendous opportunities to increase Nigeria’s competitiveness in global markets, lower transaction costs, and improve market access.

How We Operate

TCX Business Operations

Trading Platform

A robust trading platform powered by a provider trading system that enables all users (farmers, cooperatives, buyers, brokers) to trade seamlessly from anywhere in the country.

Price Discovery

Through the dissemination of data generated, the exchange provides participants with a transparent price discovery mechanism that adds confidence and enables better price negotiations.

Warehousing Operations

Value-added services including secure storage, product drying, grading, and re-packaging. Backed by collateral management services that issue electronic warehouse receipts (e-WRs) for agricultural financing.
