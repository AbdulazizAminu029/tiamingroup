About Tiamin Group

Leading Manufacturing and Agricultural Company of the 21st Century.

Tiamin Group’s diverse portfolio includes road construction, rice processing, farming, charcoal production, commodity exchange, and international gold trading in Dubai. This versatility underscores our dynamic approach and dedication to growth.

Headquartered in Kano and Bauchi, Nigeria, with a combined workforce of around 600 employees, Tiamin Group prides itself on its ethical practices, ambitious spirit, and relentless pursuit of success. We aim to be a catalyst for future growth, achieving remarkable outcomes in every endeavour, from corporate negotiations to community engagements.

Tiamin Group Stands at the Forefront of Reclaiming Africa’s Food Destiny

"Survival of the Greenest: Reclaiming Africa’s Food Destiny" is not just a theme—it is our operational mandate.

As a proud co-sponsor of this pivotal event, Tiamin Group was on the ground, not just to discuss the problems, but to present our tangible solutions. The energy at our stand was a testament to the shared commitment to this cause.

We were honoured to host and engage with key dignitaries, thought leaders, and partners who stopped by to explore synergies, discuss sustainable agro-value chains, and explore our innovative clean energy solutions like the Rice Husk Briquette.

Message from the Leadership

Committed to Driving Africa's Industrial Renaissance

"Our vision is not just to build businesses, but to architect a self-sustaining, empowered future for the continent."
