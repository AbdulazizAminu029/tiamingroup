Our Dubai Presence

Dubai has evolved over the years to become one of the renowned gold industry hubs in the world. Tiamin Gold has been a part of that advancement, greatly contributing to the glittering gold culture of Dubai.

We can say with great honour and pride that since 2017, Tiamin Gold has developed and expanded so much to be one of the fast-growing gold traders in Dubai. We are wholesale traders of fine gold bars of high purity, and our team has made us a reputable name in the trading of gold and jewellery.

We are recently generating a new concept in the Gold Industry that will enrich individuals and ensure corporate financial freedom through finding and sourcing the right, sustainable solutions to protect against economic downturns.
